# Intellectual-property clearance boundary

Status: preliminary historical risk register; not a legal opinion or freedom-
to-operate conclusion.

This repository does not claim ownership of the underlying ideas of calorie
tracking, cryptocurrency wallets, XRP Ledger integration, NFTs, decentralised
identity, IPFS, BigchainDB, food provenance, or combinations of those concepts.
Copyright may protect qualifying original expression, not the underlying ideas
and principles.

The prototype is inactive and must not be revived, packaged, or marketed until
the following are completed:

- natural-person authorship and written assignment/licence evidence;
- exact dependency resolution, SBOM, licence texts, NOTICE files, and security
  review for all source and binary components;
- visual-asset, font, screenshot, and generated-content provenance;
- trade-mark clearance for all names and logos, separate from copyright;
- claim-by-claim patent freedom-to-operate review in intended territories for
  implemented wallet, food, identity, provenance, and storage functions; and
- regulatory review for any mainnet, token, payment, custody, exchange, reward,
  financial, health, or consumer-facing functionality.

Similarity to a public project, paper, patent title, or abstract neither proves
copying nor establishes clearance. Public availability is not permission to
copy. Review actual source provenance, contractual restrictions, licence
compatibility, patent claims/status/territory, and likelihood of consumer
confusion before relying on third-party work.
